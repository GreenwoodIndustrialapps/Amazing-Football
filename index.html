Plan your moves, then score

A football game where you plan every move before your footballer runs the laser maze.

How it works

PlanChoose Left 90, Forward, Right 90 and Shoot before you enter the maze.
STEVE7
GoPress Go and watch your footballer follow your plan through the lasers.
ScoreGet the ball, face the goal, then shoot. Hit a laser wall or miss the goal and you lose it.

Each game is five goes in five different mazes. Try to score all five.

Choose a level

Practice · Free Easy · Free Medium Hard

Practice mode shows your moves as you plan them. In every other mode you do not see them in advance. Hard adds a defender and limited moves. One unlock, bought once in the App Store, opens Medium and Hard. No subscription.
