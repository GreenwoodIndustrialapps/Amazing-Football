Privacy policy

Amazing Football collects nothing about you or anyone who plays.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, receive or share any information about you or anyone who plays.

What stays on the phone

The game remembers a few things on the phone itself so it works: the name and number on the shirt, the shirt colour, the level you last chose and whether sound is on. This never leaves the phone. Deleting the game removes it.

Voice

The spoken help on the grown-ups sum page uses the voice built into your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

The unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details or your Apple account.

Who can use it

This game can be used by adults and children. It collects no personal information from anyone, and it has no links to other websites or apps.

Changes

If this ever changes, the new version will be in the game and on our website.
