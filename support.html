Common questions

How do I play?

The Home page explains it in three steps, and the game has its own Instructions page.

What does the unlock include?

Practice and Easy are free. One purchase, made once in the App Store, opens Medium and Hard. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy.

How do I buy it, or get it back on a new phone?

Choose Medium or Hard and tap Unlock. A short sum for adults opens first, so purchases are never made by accident. The unlock page has Unlock and Restore purchase. To get your purchase back, tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple account.

Does it need the internet?

No. Only buying or restoring the unlock needs a connection.

What is saved on the phone?

The name and number on the shirt, the shirt colour, the level you last chose and whether sound is on. It stays on the phone, and deleting the game removes it. Read the privacy policy.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the red speaker button at the far right of the planning, run and result screens. It is remembered.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhones with iOS 16.6 or later.
